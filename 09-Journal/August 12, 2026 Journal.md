### **. Cyber Security**
Today I did 30. SIM Swap Attacks lesson in Cyber Attacks section.
#### 1. What is a SIM Swap Attack?

A **SIM swap attack** (also called **SIM hijacking**) happens when a hacker tricks your mobile carrier into transferring your phone number to a SIM card that _they_ own.

#### 2. How the Attack Works

- **Gathering Info:** The attacker researches you online to find personal details like your date of birth, pet's name, or other security question answers.
- **Social Engineering:** They call your mobile phone provider, pretend to be you, and claim they lost their phone and need their number moved to a "new" SIM card.
- **The Takeover:** If the carrier believes them, your phone will suddenly lose service because your number has been moved to the hacker's device.
- **Stealing Access:** The hacker can now receive your **SMS-based 2FA codes** (the security texts you get when logging in). This lets them reset your passwords and access your bank or social media accounts.

#### 3. Real-World Example: Jack Dorsey

In 2019, the Twitter account of CEO **Jack Dorsey** was hijacked. Attackers stole his phone number and used it to post fraudulent messages from his official account.

#### 4. How to Protect Yourself

- **Use Authenticator Apps:** Whenever possible, use an app (like Google Authenticator) instead of SMS for security codes. These apps are much harder for a hacker to intercept.
- **Add a Carrier PIN:** Set up a special **security PIN** or code with your mobile provider. This ensures they won't make any changes to your account or move your number without that secret code.
