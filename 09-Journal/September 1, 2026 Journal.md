### **2. Cyber Security**
Today I did an incognito lesson:

#### 1. How to Open It (Quick Shortcuts)

You can quickly open an incognito window using these keyboard shortcuts:

*   **Google Chrome:** `Ctrl + Shift + N`

*   **Mozilla Firefox:** `Ctrl + Shift + P`

#### 2. What Incognito Mode Does (Local Protection)

When you close an incognito window, your browser automatically wipes the digital footprints of that session from your device.
  
*   **What is NOT saved:** Your search history, your browsing history, and any cookies created during that specific session.

*   **What IS still saved:** Any **bookmarks** you create or **files you download** while incognito will remain on your computer.

#### 3. The Big Misconception: Who Can Still Track You?

Many beginners mistake incognito mode for a "super stealth" tool that hides them from everyone. In reality, it only changes what your **local browser** remembers. The following groups can still see exactly what you are doing online:

*   **Your Internet Service Provider (ISP):** Your ISP provides your connection to the web, meaning they can still see and track all your network activity even if you are in incognito mode.

*   **Your Employer:** If you are using a computer or a network managed by your school or company, they can still monitor your activity. For example, opening YouTube in an incognito window does not hide your browsing from your employer's monitoring systems.

*   **Websites You Log Into:** If you open an incognito window and log into an account (like Facebook), that website immediately knows exactly who you are and will associate your activity with your account.

*   **Keyloggers & Security Software:** If your computer has security software or has been infected with malicious tools like **keyloggers**, they can record your keystrokes and monitor your activity regardless of whether you are in incognito mode.

*   **Browser Extensions:** Some browser extensions are allowed to run in private browsing and may continue to monitor or operate while you browse.

### **Incognito Mode Quick-Recall Table**

Use this quick cheat sheet to study what is protected and what is still exposed:

| What is being tracked? | Is it hidden by Incognito Mode? | Why or why not? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Browsing/Search History** | ✅ **Yes** (Usually not saved) | The browser forgets the sites you visited once you close the window. |
| **Session Cookies** | ✅ **Yes** (Removed after) | Cookies are deleted so websites don't remember you on your next visit. |
| **Bookmarks & Downloads** | ❌ **No** (Still saved) | These are permanent changes you made to your files/browser. |
| **Your ISP** | ❌ **No** (Can still see) | Your internet provider still routes your traffic and records your destination. |
| **Your Employer/School** | ❌ **No** (Can still monitor) | Network administrators can watch traffic passing through their systems. |
| **Accounts You Log Into** | ❌ **No** (Can identify you) | The moment you type in your login, the website connects your actions to your profile. |
| **Keyloggers/Malware** | ❌ **No** (Can still monitor) | Malicious software on your device watches your screen/keyboard directly. |
