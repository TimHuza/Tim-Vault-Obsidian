#### 1. Privacy (Control Over Your Info)

**Privacy** is not about hiding completely from the world. Instead, it is about **having control over your personal information**.

- It does **not** mean that absolutely nobody else can ever access your details.
- It means **you** get to decide **who** can see your information and **what** they are allowed to do with it.
- **Real-World Example:** You share your private medical details with your doctor so they can treat you. You trust them to keep it private and not share it with the public.
- **Another Example:** Using encrypted email services so only you and the recipient can read the contents of your messages.
- **Key Idea:** **Privacy = Control.** 🔒

#### 2. Anonymity (Hiding Who You Are)

**Anonymity** means your **true identity is completely hidden**.

- When you are anonymous, other people cannot connect your online actions back to your real-world identity.
- **Real-World Example:** Posting a comment on an online forum as a "guest" without creating an account or typing in your name.
- **Another Example:** Using privacy-focused web tools like **Tor** or a VPN (while avoiding logging into personal accounts) to make it harder to trace your network activity back to your real identity.
- **The Hard Truth:** Achieving **100% anonymity online is extremely difficult** today. Modern systems can track and identify you using advanced techniques like browser fingerprinting, metadata collection, and AI-powered correlation of information.
- **Key Idea:** **Anonymity = Hiding who you are.** 🕵️

#### 3. Pseudonymity (Using a Consistent Fake Name)

**Pseudonymity** means you consistently use a **false identity or fake name** online.

- The big difference between this and anonymity is **consistency**. Under anonymity, you don't have an identity at all. Under pseudonymity, you _do_ have an identity, but it is a **fake one** that you use over and over.
- **Real-World Example:** A popular YouTuber who uses a fake username and avatar, never showing their real face or sharing their real name.
- **The Risk of Pseudonymity:** If anyone ever discovers your real identity, **all of your past online activities** under that fake name can suddenly be linked directly back to you.
- Attackers or investigators can connect your fake name to your real name by analyzing your writing style, online behavior, personal opinions, or the topics you talk about most.
- **Key Idea:** **Pseudonymity = Using a consistent fake identity.** 🎭
